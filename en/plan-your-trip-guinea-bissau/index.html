Flights to Bissau

Choose your international flights to Bissau according to your dates and departure airport. Send us your flight times before booking your transfer: we will organise your airport welcome and onward journey to the lodge around tides and navigation conditions.

Visa and passport

An entry visa is required. In France, applications are made through the Embassy of Guinea-Bissau in Paris. Check with the embassy for the entry conditions and passport validity required for your nationality.

Health and vaccinations

Yellow fever vaccination is compulsory and must be given at an approved centre. A certificate for a first vaccination becomes valid 10 days after vaccination. Malaria is present: protect yourself against mosquitoes and ask your doctor about preventive treatment. Consult a travel health doctor a few weeks before leaving. Only drink bottled water or water that has been made safe to drink.

Insurance

Insurance covering medical expenses and medical repatriation is essential. The country has no emergency service equipped for serious accidents, and the nearest air ambulance is in Dakar, approximately 1 hour 30 minutes away by air.

This flight time is not a guaranteed evacuation timeframe.

When to visit

The lodge is open from October to the end of May, during the dry season, the best time to discover the archipelago. To see turtles on Poilão, the ideal period is from October to mid-February.

Money and payment

The islands operate on cash only, in CFA francs. There are no ATMs in the archipelago: bring enough cash before leaving Bissau. Exchanging euros for CFA francs may be possible locally, but cannot be guaranteed.

Electricity and wifi at the lodge

Electricity at the lodge comes from solar panels, supplemented by a generator that powers the air conditioning. The rooms have a fan and air conditioning. The sockets are the same as in France: French travellers do not need an adapter. Wifi is provided through Starlink.

Safety and support

Your safety is part of our planning. Your transfers and excursions take place with our boats and our local team, who know the tides and islands. You have a contact available on WhatsApp at every stage. We monitor local developments and keep you informed before departure. Before leaving, consult your country's travel advice and, if you are French, register with Ariane.

National parks

Orango and João Vieira-Poilão are national parks managed by IBAP, the Institute for Biodiversity and Protected Areas. For a visit, allow for park entry from CFA 20,000 per person; an IBAP guide, CFA 15,000; and boat hire, approximately CFA 320,000 shared between the participants. The larger the group, the lower the cost per person: we give you the exact total in your stay proposal.

Respecting Bijagó culture

Some places are sacred and reserved for initiated members of the community, including the island of Poilão: never enter without your guide's permission. Always ask before photographing a person, a child or a ceremony. Dress appropriately in villages. Do not give money or sweets to children: if you would like to help, we can direct you to the village school.

What to bring

Local shops are very basic, selling snacks, water, rice and everyday drinks. Bring sunscreen, mosquito repellent, toiletries and personal items, as well as your medicines in their original packaging with the prescription. Shampoo and shower gel are provided at the lodge.

The rhythm of the island

Here, life follows nature, the tides and a different rhythm. Stay flexible and enjoy the experience to the full. We look forward to welcoming you to Cajou Lodge.
